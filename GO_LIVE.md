# Going live — note for Mr Briggs

**What it does:** Answers your traders on WhatsApp 24/7. Tells each trader their balance, gives them a checkout link to pay with, confirms payments automatically (no screenshots), takes reorders only within their credit limit, records promises to pay and stock left in their shop. Gives you a scorecard of who pays fast, who sells fast, and who is lagging, and suggests credit-limit changes for you to approve.

**What it doesn't do:** It never gives discounts, forgives debt, raises a limit, or settles a dispute — those come to you. It doesn't arrange delivery. Sell-through is only as good as traders' stock reports.

**Before real customers:**
1. Paystack live keys and a verified business account; dedicated virtual accounts enabled for your business.
2. A WhatsApp Business number connected to Chord, plus approved message templates if we send reminders first.
3. Your real trader list, phones and limits loaded into the sheet; check every opening balance with you.
4. Who gets alerted: you (and one backup staff) by email/WhatsApp when a trader disputes, a payment can't be found, or the system errors.
5. Failed payments: the trader gets a fresh link; nothing is marked paid until Paystack confirms. Refunds stay manual.
6. A two-week pilot with 5 trusted traders, and a weekly check that the sheet matches your bank statement.
7. Identity: today the agent knows who it is talking to only from the phone number the person gives in the chat; Chord doesn't yet send our system a verified sender number. So in the test setup, someone who types another trader's number, or yours, is treated as that person. Before real customers we connect the real WhatsApp number (where the sender is verified by WhatsApp) and require your PIN for every owner action, such as changing a limit.
